# Setra go-to-market

How businesses find Setra, start using it, pay, stay, and bring others. Written for where Setra is
today: live on Monad testnet, no paying customers yet, no paid marketing budget.

## Who we sell to first

Small businesses already paid in USDC, starting on Monad:

- agencies, studios and freelancers with clients abroad, paid in stablecoins to avoid wire fees and delays;
- online stores that accept stablecoins at checkout (Shopify and WooCommerce);
- software and service companies selling to crypto-native customers.

The person who buys is the owner or whoever does the books. Their words for the problem: "Who sent
this?" and "I just see an amount and a wallet address."

**Is the problem worth marketing?** It shows up with every payment (high frequency) and becomes
expensive at month end, when someone reconciles by hand and customers get chased for invoices they
already paid (meaningful size). Frequent and costly is the quadrant where word of mouth and content
compound, which shapes everything below.

**Who we don't sell to (yet):** funds and traders who need tax-lot accounting, and large finance
teams that buy through procurement. Cryptio and Bitwave serve them.

## Acquisition: how strangers find us

| Move | How it works | Number we watch |
|---|---|---|
| **The Monad ecosystem first** | Founders in Metropolis and the Monad community who invoice clients in USDC. Direct outreach from the founder, with a free setup call. | Conversations booked per week |
| **Every invoice is an ad** | Each payment page says "Powered by Setra", and the person paying is often a business too. When they ask how to get the same, the answer is one click away. | Sign-ups that start from a payment page |
| **Accountants and bookkeepers** | One bookkeeper who serves crypto businesses brings many clients. They get a free workspace for their own practice and see all their clients' books in the format they already use (QuickBooks and Xero exports). | Clients per partner |
| **Store app listings** | Setra already takes orders from Shopify and WooCommerce. Listing in their app stores reaches merchants the moment they decide to accept stablecoins. | Installs, then first matched order |
| **Guides people search for** | Plain-language answers to the questions owners type into Google and ChatGPT: how to record a USDC payment, how to handle sales tax on a stablecoin sale, how to reconcile a wallet. Each guide also becomes a post and a sales answer. | Visits to sign-ups |

We skip paid ads until we know what a customer is worth and costs to win.

## Activation: the first five minutes

The goal is one moment: **a real payment matched to an invoice and in the books.**

- Free to start, no card. Paste a wallet address, send yourself an invoice, pay it with testnet USDC, and watch it reach the books.
- Store owners connect Shopify or WooCommerce instead and see their next order arrive by itself.
- Seti greets them with what needs attention, so the next step is always obvious.

**Number we watch:** the share of new workspaces with a matched payment within 24 hours.

## Retention: why they stay

- **Closing the month.** Setra lists what's left before a month can close, then locks it. A business that closes three months in Setra has its books there, and its accountant works from them.
- **Seti in the daily routine.** "What should I focus on today?" turns the books into a habit, not a chore.
- **Their accountant depends on it.** Exports, the journal and books published on Monad make Setra the place the numbers come from.

**Number we watch:** businesses that close a month in Setra, month over month.

## Referral: how customers bring customers

- The payment page loop above: every invoice a customer sends shows Setra to another business.
- Accountant partners bring their clients.
- Founding customers who refer another business get an extra month free.

**Number we watch:** the share of new workspaces that came from a payment page or a partner.

## Revenue: how we make money

A monthly subscription set by payments received, and never a percentage of the money:

| Plan | Price | For |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0, up to 20 payments a month | Trying it, very small sellers |
| Starter | $49 a month, up to 200 payments | Freelancers and studios |
| Growth | $149 a month, up to 2,000 payments | Agencies and online stores |
| Scale | Custom, above 2,000 | High volume |

Two months free when paying yearly. The first 20 businesses get half price for a year and hands-on
setup. A card processor takes a slice of every sale; Setra charges for the software, so the more a
business earns, the better the deal gets.

## Metrics

- **North star:** payments matched per month across all businesses. It grows when businesses join, stay and grow.
- **Activation:** matched payment within 24 hours of sign-up.
- **Retention:** months closed in Setra.
- **Referral:** sign-ups from payment pages and partners.
- **Revenue:** paying businesses and monthly recurring revenue.

## Open questions

- **What it costs to win a customer.** Unknown until the founder-led pilots run. Paid channels wait for this number.
- **Willingness to pay.** $49 and $149 are informed by what owners pay for QuickBooks, Xero and Request Finance, and will be tested with founding customers.
- **Mainnet timing.** Live payments on Monad mainnet start with the founding customers, once the testnet pilots confirm the flow.
